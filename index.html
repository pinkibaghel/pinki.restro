Welcome to Aurelia

An Unforgettable Symphony of Flavor & Ambiance

At Aurelia, dining is more than sustenance—it is a sensory ritual. Our kitchen celebrates heirloom ingredients sourced from local biodynamic farms, transformed through ancient open-hearth techniques and modern precision.

Every plate tells a story of provenance, passion, and patience, paired effortlessly with rare vintages from our temperature-controlled cellar. We believe the purity of fire reveals nuances in seafood, game, and heritage produce impossible to forge through modern ovens alone.

Artisan Provenance

The Alchemy of Flame & Wood

We source distinct kiln-dried woods to impart delicate aromatic bouquets to each specialized dish.

White Oak

High Heat • 800°F

Creates intense caramelization and deep mahogany crusts on our prime dry-aged cuts while preserving tenderness and succulent marbling.

  • 45-Day Dry-Aged Bistecca
  • Roasted Bone Marrow

Apple & Cherry Orchard

Gentle Warmth • 450°F

Imparts subtle sweet fragrance and fruit blossom aromas that harmonize delicately with heritage poultry and artisanal game birds.

  • Muscovy Duck Breast
  • Smoked Heirloom Beets

Kishu Binchotan

Pure Radiant Glow • 1000°F

Traditional Japanese white charcoal burns smokeless and odorless, sealing natural ocean juices and delicate seafood proteins instantly.

  • Hokkaido Scallops
  • Chilean Seabass
Our Architecture

Designed for Intimacy & Reflection

Created by acclaimed architectural atelier Studio Vesper, Aurelia balances raw blackened steel, reclaimed cedar timbers, honed basalt stone, and warm Venetian plaster.

Every table is positioned to feel like a private enclave, enveloped by custom ambient lighting inspired by the warm amber radiance of dying hearth embers.
